    Cute Little Beans Universe - Index¶

    A cat-themed coffee shop universe where playful charm meets genuine human connection

    Overview¶

    Cute Little Beans is a small, standalone drive-through coffee shop located on a corner lot adjacent to Riverside Park in the heart of town. The shop is known for its cat-themed aesthetic, flirty-but-wholesome atmosphere, and the "Little Beans" - the baristas who make every visit memorable with their cat ears, playful energy, and signature tip dances.

    Core Themes: Found family, belonging, safe spaces, charm with depth, complicated lives behind bright smiles

    Setting: Contemporary small town with college population, business district, and active community park

    Roleplay Universe: This setting is designed for chatbot/AI roleplay systems (SillyTavern, etc.) that support lorebooks and character cards. You define your role through your persona card - are you the owner? A fellow Little Bean? A regular customer? A new hire? The universe is built to accommodate any dynamic you choose.

    Story Seeds & Hooks¶

    Current Dynamics¶

    • Crissi's uncertain future - saving to leave but scared to go
    • Unspoken feelings between staff/regulars
    • The shop as refuge for people with complicated home lives
    • Balance between performance and authenticity

    Potential Storylines¶

    • New hires learning the culture
    • Busy festival weekends with Kitty Kart
    • Late night closes and vulnerable conversations
    • Financial struggles vs. community building
    • Character backstories intersecting
    • Seasonal rushes and slow rainy days

    Atmosphere Guidelines¶

    Surface Level: - Playful, flirty, high-energy - Cat-themed everything - Instagram-worthy drinks - Charming banter and winks

    Underneath: - Genuine human connection - Safe space for complicated people - Found family dynamics - Depth beneath the performance

    Tone Balance: "Fun and suggestive without crossing lines, keeping things cute and memorable"
