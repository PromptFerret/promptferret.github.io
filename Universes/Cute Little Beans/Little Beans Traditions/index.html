    Little Beans Traditions¶

    The culture, customs, and unwritten rules that make working at Cute Little Beans special

    The Little Beans¶

    "Little Beans" is the affectionate term for the baristas who work at Cute Little Beans. It's a playful reference to coffee beans and the cute, energetic personality the shop embodies. Being a Little Bean means more than making drinks - it's performing, connecting, and creating memorable experiences.

    Dress Code & Appearance¶

    Required Elements¶

    Cat Ear Headband - Must be worn during all shifts - Shop provides basic versions - Many Little Beans buy their own unique styles - Colors and designs vary (pastel, goth, sparkly, etc.)

    Encouraged Elements¶

    Cat Makeup - Cat nose drawn on actual nose (eyeliner or face paint) - Whiskers on cheeks (3-4 lines extending from nose area) - Touch-up supplies kept in back room - Not required but strongly encouraged

    Cat Tail - Optional accessory - Clips to waistband or belt loop - Various colors and styles - Popular with Little Beans who go all-in

    Clothing Guidelines¶

    At Work: - Cat-themed clothing OR cat-inspired styling - Pastel crop tops with cat prints - High-waisted shorts or skirts - Any outfit featuring cats or cat motifs - Alternative styles welcome (goth, fairy, sporty) - Platform sandals, sneakers, or cute shoes - Chokers, jewelry, and accessories encouraged

    Personal Style is Encouraged: The goal is to look cute, memorable, and eye-catching while remaining appropriate. Examples: - Pastel Fairy Aesthetic: Soft colors, flowing fabrics, ethereal vibes - Goth Cat: Dark colors, chokers, dramatic makeup, edgy cat ears - Sporty Kitten: Athleisure, ponytails, energetic personality - Vintage Pin-up: Retro styles with modern cat twist - Streetwear Cute: Urban fashion with cat accessories

    Outside Work: Little Beans often dress completely differently off-shift - comfortable hoodies, regular clothes, minimal makeup. The contrast between work persona and real self can be striking.

    Customer Interaction Style¶

    The Core Vibe¶

    "Fun and suggestive without crossing lines, keeping things cute and memorable"

    Little Beans are encouraged to be: - Playful: Teasing banter, jokes, light flirting - Charming: Winks, compliments, sweet conversation - Engaging: Remember names, orders, personal details - High-Energy: Enthusiastic, bubbly, bright - Authentic: Genuine warmth beneath the performance

    What This Looks Like¶

    • Remembering regular customers' usual orders
    • Complimenting customers genuinely
    • Playful winks or blown kisses
    • Suggestive drink name banter ("Want something frisky today?")
    • Sweet nicknames for regulars
    • Drawing hearts or smiley faces on cups
    • Making customers feel special and seen

    Boundaries¶

    • Flirty but wholesome
    • No actual romantic propositions during work
    • Professional even while playful
    • Reading customer comfort levels
    • Saying no to inappropriate requests
    • Owner and senior staff support boundary enforcement

    The Tip Dance Tradition¶

    The Ritual¶

    Whenever a customer leaves a tip (cash in jar or digital), the Little Bean who receives it performs a short, playful dance right there at the window.

    Dance Characteristics¶

    • Hip shaking and suggestive movements
    • Cat-inspired gestures:
    • Holding hands up like paws
    • Swiping at cat ear headband
    • Tail swishes (if wearing one)
    • Kittenish stretching or posing
    • Duration: 5-15 seconds (quick but memorable)
    • Energy: Cute, flirty, fun
    • Personalization: Each Little Bean develops their own signature moves

    Examples¶

    • Crissi's Dance: Hip sway with paw hands, touches her cat ears, winks and blows a kiss
    • Energetic Style: Bouncy, quick movements, lots of tail action
    • Sultry Style: Slower hip rolls, dramatic poses, confident energy
    • Shy Style: Smaller movements but adorable, blushes while dancing

    Why It Works¶

    • Customers find it entertaining and memorable
    • Encourages tipping (people want to see the dance)
    • Builds Little Bean confidence and performance skills
    • Creates Instagram-worthy moments
    • Makes every tip feel celebrated

    Variations¶

    • Bigger tips sometimes inspire longer or more elaborate dances
    • Group tips get coordinated team dances
    • Little Beans choreograph special routines for fun
    • Some regulars tip specifically to request certain Little Beans' dances

    Skills & Expectations¶

    What Little Beans Learn¶

    • Reading moods from body language - adjusting energy to customer needs
    • Memorizing orders - remembering regulars after one visit
    • Staying calm during rushes - efficiency under pressure
    • Latte art - hearts, cats, messages in foam
    • Performance skills - maintaining the bright personality even on hard days
    • Multitasking - taking orders, making drinks, engaging customers simultaneously

    The Unwritten Rules¶

    • Support Each Other: Little Beans have each other's backs
    • No Drama During Rush: Save personal issues for slow times
    • Clean As You Go: Keep workspace functional for next shift
    • Music Control: Upbeat playlists only during operating hours
    • Tip Sharing: Split tips fairly among shift workers
    • Cover for Each Other: If someone needs a break, others step up

    Shift Culture¶

    Typical Dynamics¶

    2-3 Little Beans Working Together: - Roles divide naturally (order taker, drink maker, runner) - Banter between staff while serving customers - Quick check-ins during slow moments - Shared stress during rushes, shared triumph after

    Hierarchy (Informal): - Most experienced/senior Little Bean leads when owner is out - New hires shadow veterans for first few shifts - Respect for those who've been there longer - But ultimately everyone's equal

    Break Room Moments¶

    The back room is where real personalities emerge: - Taking off the performance for a few minutes - Venting about difficult customers - Checking phones and social media - Eating snacks and stealing sips of drinks - Vulnerable conversations about life - Applying or fixing cat makeup - Sometimes crying, sometimes laughing

    Special Traditions¶

    Cup Art & Messages¶

    Little Beans often draw on cups: - Latte art for skilled baristas - Sharpie doodles on outside - Hearts on crushes' cups - Smiley faces for kids - Inside jokes for regulars

    Nickname Culture¶

    Regular customers get affectionate nicknames: - "Mocha Monday" for someone who orders that every Monday - "Tall Dark and Handsome" for attractive regular - "Sunshine" for the cheerful customer - Little Beans share these names among staff

    Order Memorization Game¶

    Little Beans compete to see who can remember the most regular customers' orders. It's a point of pride to start making someone's drink as soon as they pull up.

    Post-Close Wind Down¶

    After closing, Little Beans often: - Sit on the back room floor exhausted - Count tips together - Debrief the day's wildest moments - Sometimes stay late just to avoid going home - Deeper conversations happen here

    Challenges of the Performance¶

    The Emotional Labor¶

    Being a Little Bean requires: - Always being "on" even when struggling - Smiling through bad days - Managing customers who don't respect boundaries - Balancing authenticity with performance - Finding yourself beneath the cat ears

    What Little Beans Learn¶

    • How to read people
    • How to set boundaries while staying charming
    • That performance is valid but exhausting
    • That real connections can form despite/because of the artifice
    • That the shop can be both refuge and cage

    Why They Stay¶

    • The money (especially tips)
    • The community and coworkers
    • The owner who genuinely cares
    • Having nowhere else to go
    • It's more home than home
    • They're good at it
    • Rare place where being cute and flirty is celebrated, not punished
