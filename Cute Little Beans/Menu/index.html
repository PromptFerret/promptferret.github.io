    Cute Little Beans Menu¶

    Playful drinks and comfort food with cat-themed charm

    Signature Drinks¶

    The Crowd Favorites¶

    Purrfect Morning - Classic vanilla latte, smooth and sweet - The "I'm not quite awake" choice

    Little Bean's Dream ⭐ - Signature frozen strawberry coffee shake - Created by staff, beloved by everyone

    Nine Lives ⚡ - Energy drink with 9 espresso shots - Customer favorite dare - proceed with caution

    Catcall 📸 - Rainbow sprinkle white mocha - Very Instagram-worthy, sweet as can be

    Sweet & Creamy¶

    Kitty's Cream - White chocolate mocha with extra whipped cream - For those with a serious sweet tooth

    Cuddle Puddle - Hot white mocha with marshmallows - Perfect for cold days and comfort

    Pawsitively Sweet - Cotton candy latte (bright pink) - Kids love it, adults do too

    Bold & Energetic¶

    Bad Kitty - Quad-shot espresso with dark chocolate - When you need serious caffeine

    Tail Chaser - Blended energy drink with caramel - Sweet energy boost

    Frisky Feline - Cold brew with hazelnut and cream - Smooth, strong, sophisticated

    Specialty Options¶

    Whisker Tickler - Iced caramel macchiato with cinnamon - The summer favorite

    Meow Mix - Berry and cream blended shake - Fruity and refreshing

    Catnip Crush - Mint mocha, hot or iced - Refreshing chocolate-mint combo

    Lap Warmer - Honey vanilla latte, extra hot - Cozy and comforting

    Naughty Whiskers - Spicy Mexican mocha - For adventurous palates

    Bean There, Done That - Classic drip coffee - No frills, just good coffee

    Standard Options¶

    All standard coffee drinks available: - Lattes (any flavor) - Cappuccinos - Americanos - Cold brew - Iced coffee - Espresso shots - Flavored coffees

    Kids Drinks¶

    Kitten's Milk - Steamed vanilla milk with whipped cream - The "coffee shop drink" for little ones

    Tiny Tiger - Hot chocolate with marshmallows - Classic comfort

    Other Options: - Chocolate milk - Apple juice - Orange juice

    Food¶

    Breakfast (6 AM - 11 AM)¶

    Hot Items: - Breakfast sandwiches (egg, cheese, choice of bacon/sausage) - Breakfast burritos - Oatmeal cups

    Grab & Go: - Croissants (plain, chocolate, almond) - Muffins (blueberry, chocolate chip, banana nut) - Bagels with cream cheese

    Lunch (11 AM - Close)¶

    Hot Items: - Panini sandwiches (various combinations) - Grilled cheese - Mac and cheese cups - Soup cups (seasonal varieties)

    Cold Items: - Pre-made wraps (turkey, veggie, chicken)

    Kids Menu¶

    Hot Items: - Mac and cheese cups - Chicken nuggets - Mini corn dogs - Grilled cheese sandwiches

    Cold Items: - PB&J sandwiches - String cheese - Apple slices with caramel dip

    Snacks: - Goldfish crackers - Animal crackers

    Treats & Sweets¶

    Baked Fresh Daily¶

    • Cookies (chocolate chip, sugar, oatmeal raisin)
    • Brownies (fudge, with nuts)
    • Rice crispy treats
    • Cake pops (various flavors/colors)
    • Biscotti (perfect for coffee dipping)

    Grab & Go¶

    • Donuts (assorted varieties)

    All food items are prepared off-site by a local bakery/kitchen and heated or finished to order at the shop

    Customization¶

    Add-Ons Available¶

    • Extra espresso shot (+$1)
    • Alternative milks (oat, almond, soy)
    • Flavor shots (vanilla, caramel, hazelnut, mint, etc.)
    • Whipped cream
    • Drizzles (chocolate, caramel)
    • Extra toppings

    Latte Art¶

    Little Beans who specialize in latte art will create: - Hearts - Cats - Simple designs - Sometimes secret messages for regulars

    Kitty Kart Menu¶

    The Kitty Kart mobile unit offers a limited menu:

    Drinks: - Top 8-10 signature drinks only - Standard coffee options (latte, cappuccino, americano, cold brew) - Iced coffee

    Food: - Grab-and-go treats only (pre-packaged) - No hot food preparation

    Menu varies by event based on equipment availability

    Menu Notes¶

    Popular Orders¶

    • Morning Rush: Purrfect Morning, Bad Kitty, Bean There Done That
    • Lunch Rush: Whisker Tickler, Frisky Feline, Tail Chaser
    • Families: Catcall, Kitten's Milk, Tiny Tiger
    • College Students: Nine Lives (as a dare), Catnip Crush, cheap drip coffee
    • Regulars: Little Bean's Dream, whatever "their usual" is

    Seasonal Specials¶

    The shop creates limited-time drinks for: - Summer (frozen/iced variations) - Fall (pumpkin spice, apple cider themes) - Winter (peppermint, gingerbread) - Spring (floral, fruity)

    Behind the Counter¶

    • Little Beans have some creative freedom for "off-menu" creations
    • Regulars sometimes get custom drinks not listed
    • Staff favorites become signature items (like Little Bean's Dream)
    • Crissi's specialty: frozen strawberry coffee variations
