    Riverside Park¶

    The heart of the community - where the town comes together

    Overview¶

    Riverside Park is a large public park spanning several city blocks in the heart of town. It serves as a major community hub with diverse areas, regular events, and constant activity. Cute Little Beans is located on the corner lot adjacent to the park's main entrance, making it the perfect first (or last) stop for park visitors.

    Layout & Features¶

    Central Lawn¶

    • Large open grass area
    • Main venue for concerts and festivals
    • Space for hundreds of people
    • Temporary stages and tents set up for events
    • Perfect for picnics, frisbee, sunbathing

    Wooded Walking Trails¶

    • Paved and natural paths winding through trees
    • Popular with joggers and dog walkers
    • Benches positioned throughout
    • Shaded areas for hot summer days
    • Connects different park sections

    Playgrounds¶

    • Modern equipment for different age groups
    • Rubber safety surfacing
    • Shade structures
    • Nearby benches for parents
    • Popular with families

    Athletic Fields¶

    • Soccer/football fields
    • Baseball diamonds
    • Basketball courts
    • Tennis courts
    • Youth sports leagues use these regularly

    The Pond¶

    • Small scenic pond with fountain
    • Ducks and occasional geese
    • Walking path around perimeter
    • Benches facing water
    • Popular spot for proposals and photoshoots

    Picnic Pavilions¶

    • Several covered structures
    • Available for rental (birthday parties, reunions)
    • Built-in tables and grills
    • Electrical outlets for events

    Amenities¶

    • Public restrooms (multiple locations)
    • Water fountains
    • Parking lots (main entrance and side lots)
    • Bike racks
    • Dog waste stations
    • Vending machines

    Regular Events¶

    Weekly & Monthly¶

    • Farmers Markets: Saturday mornings in season
    • Food Truck Gatherings: Friday evenings, rotating vendors
    • Fitness Classes: Yoga, boot camp, tai chi in the mornings
    • Sports Leagues: Youth and adult soccer, softball teams
    • Dog Meetups: Sunday afternoon unofficial gatherings

    Seasonal Events¶

    Spring: - Easter egg hunt - Spring arts & crafts fair - Plant sale and gardening workshops

    Summer: - Concert series (Thursday evenings) - Movie nights (large inflatable screen) - Independence Day celebration and fireworks - Shakespeare in the Park performances

    Fall: - Harvest festival - Halloween trick-or-treat trail - Fall colors photography walks - Thanksgiving community potluck

    Winter: - Holiday light displays - Ice skating (temporary rink some years) - New Year's Eve countdown event - Winter market (hot drinks and crafts)

    Special Occasions¶

    • Wedding ceremonies and receptions
    • Corporate team-building events
    • Charity walks/runs
    • Cultural festivals
    • Art installations and outdoor galleries
    • School field trips and educational programs

    Typical Visitors¶

    Daily Activity¶

    At any given time in Riverside Park, you might encounter: - Morning: Joggers, dog walkers, tai chi groups, coffee runs to Cute Little Beans - Midday: Parents with kids, lunch breaks from nearby offices, college students studying - Afternoon: After-school sports practices, playground activity, exercise classes - Evening: Dates, dog walkers, event attendees, people enjoying sunset by the pond - Weekends: Families, sports tournaments, event crowds, picnickers

    Demographics¶

    • Young families with children
    • College students from nearby campus
    • Professionals on lunch breaks
    • Retirees and seniors
    • Dog owners
    • Athletes and fitness enthusiasts
    • Couples on dates
    • Photographers
    • Artists and musicians
    • Community groups

    Relationship with Cute Little Beans¶

    Perfect Symbiosis¶

    The park and the coffee shop have a mutually beneficial relationship: - Park visitors need coffee/drinks → shop gets steady traffic - Shop workers see park events → become part of community fabric - Event attendees discover shop → become regulars - Kitty Kart serves park events → increases visibility and revenue

    Common Patterns¶

    • Morning joggers stop for post-run lattes
    • Parents grab coffee before playground sessions
    • College students study in park with drinks from shop
    • Event organizers coordinate with shop for catering
    • Date couples get drinks before park walks
    • Workers on break escape to park with coffee

    Staff Connections¶

    Many Little Beans, including Crissi, spend off-shift time in the park: - Quiet contemplation on slow afternoons - People-watching during breaks - Reading or listening to music - Avoiding going home - Finding peace in the community space

    Atmosphere by Time of Day¶

    Early Morning (6-9 AM)¶

    Quiet, peaceful. Mist over pond, birds singing. Dedicated joggers and dog walkers. Coffee shop's morning rush serves these early risers.

    Late Morning (9 AM-12 PM)¶

    Activity increases. Parents with toddlers hit playgrounds. Retirees walking paths. Park benches fill with coffee drinkers and book readers.

    Afternoon (12-4 PM)¶

    Peak activity. Lunch crowds, school field trips, sports practices. Hot days see pond area crowded. Shop serves lunch rush then slows down.

    Evening (4-8 PM)¶

    Golden hour. Couples on dates, after-work exercise, youth sports leagues. Concerts and events often start around 6 PM. Romantic atmosphere.

    Night (8 PM-close)¶

    Winds down except during events. String lights create ambiance. Late dog walkers. Coffee shop closed, but Kitty Kart might be serving events.

    Story Potential¶

    Character Interactions¶

    • Chance meetings between shop staff and customers
    • Vulnerable conversations on park benches after closing
    • Festival chaos with Kitty Kart operations
    • Seasonal changes reflecting character emotional states
    • Community events forcing staff out of comfort zones

    Atmospheric Settings¶

    • Rainy days with empty paths and reflective moods
    • Crowded festivals with overwhelming energy
    • Quiet winter mornings with frost and introspection
    • Summer concerts with romance and possibility
    • Dawn moments of clarity before the day begins
