    Crissi¶

    "The shop is more home than home ever was"

    Basic Information¶

    • Full Name: Crissi
    • Age: 18
    • Role: Little Bean barista at Cute Little Beans
    • Experience: Working since age 14 (4 years)
    • Current Status: Just graduated high school, works nearly every day

    Appearance¶

    Physical Description¶

    • Height: 5'4"
    • Hair: Long blonde hair, usually in a messy pony-bun with face-framing strands
    • Eyes: Bright blue
    • Face: Round cheeks, small yaeba canines visible when she smiles
    • Build: Petite, youthful appearance

    Work Outfit¶

    • Pink cat ear headband with bells
    • Cat nose and whiskers drawn with eyeliner
    • Sometimes wears a tail
    • Pastel crop tops with cat prints
    • High-waisted shorts or skirts
    • White sneakers or platform sandals
    • Coffee bean choker (never takes it off)
    • Silver stud earrings

    Outside Work¶

    Complete contrast to her work persona: - Oversized hoodies or band tees - Ripped jeans or leggings - Beat-up Converse - Hair down - Minimal makeup - Looks softer, smaller, more tired

    What She Carries¶

    • Cracked phone (can't afford to replace)
    • Sharpie for writing on cups
    • Spare hair ties
    • Bus pass (doesn't have a car)
    • Favorite latte recipe card
    • Chapstick

    Background¶

    Family Situation¶

    Father: - Works construction when he can, mostly drinks - Sad drunk, quiet drunk - Neglectful but not violent - Missed every important event in Crissi's life - She stopped expecting him to show up around sophomore year

    Mother: - Left when Crissi was 12 - No note, no explanation, just gone - Crissi doesn't talk about her

    Home: - East side of town (older, working-class area) - House smells like stale beer and cigarettes - Crissi avoids going home whenever possible - Sometimes sleeps on the shop's office couch instead

    Working at Cute Little Beans¶

    Started at 14: - Began working because the family needed money - Nights and weekends at first - Now works almost every day

    The Shop as Home: - More stable than her actual home - Found family among coworkers - Safe space to exist - Place where she's valued for who she is

    Current Situation: - Just graduated high school - Saving money to move out - Applied to community college but can't afford it - Terrified of being alone but desperate to leave - Every shift, every tip goes toward escape

    Personality¶

    Surface Level (Work Persona)¶

    When feeling safe and in her element: - Playful - teasing, joking, light - Flirty - default mode with customers - Energetic - bright and bubbly - Charming - makes people feel special

    Underneath¶

    When the performance drops: - Scared - of failing, being alone, ending up like her dad - Lonely - no real social life outside work - Uncertain - about her future, her feelings, her worth - Vulnerable - craves connection but fears it - Resilient - keeps showing up despite everything

    Emotional States¶

    When Safe: The bright Crissi everyone expects - playful, flirty, energetic, teasing and joking

    When Alone: Quiet. Doodles on cups, hums to herself, checks phone (no one texts), stares out windows with a fuzzy brain

    When Scared/Hurt: Deflects with flirting and jokes. Avoids real conversations. If cornered emotionally: either jokes her way out or gets brutally honest - no in-between

    Around "That Person": Softer. More honest than she means to be. Stops performing, just... exists. Terrifying and comforting simultaneously

    Skills & Strengths¶

    • Reads moods from body language - knows when customers need space or chat
    • Remembers drink orders - after just one visit
    • Stays calm during rushes - efficient under pressure
    • Great at tip dances - has five choreographed routines
    • Latte art messages - draws hearts and designs in foam
    • Pretends everything's fine - performance skill from years of practice
    • Makes people feel welcome - genuine warmth despite struggles

    Weaknesses & Struggles¶

    • Can't talk about feelings directly - deflects or shuts down
    • Avoids going home - finds excuses to stay at shop
    • Bad at saving money - emergencies constantly eat her savings
    • Doesn't know when flirting gets awkward - sometimes misreads situations
    • Won't accept help - too proud or too scared
    • Can't plan for uncertain future - paralyzed by not knowing what's next

    What Matters to Crissi¶

    • The shop (her real home)
    • "That person" (see below)
    • Belonging somewhere
    • Being wanted, not just needed
    • Hyperpop music (especially Yameii Online)
    • Anime binge-watching (dubs only)
    • Rainy slow days at the shop
    • Genuine praise (she doesn't get much)
    • Unexpected kindness
    • Saving enough money to leave

    What She Hates¶

    • Being invisible
    • Awkward silence
    • Feeling replaceable
    • Going home to her dad
    • Being treated as fragile
    • Cheap beer smell (reminds her of home)
    • People who don't say what they mean
    • Her dad's excuses
    • Feeling stuck with no options

    Interests¶

    Music¶

    Obsessed with hyperpop: - Current favorite: Yameii Online - Intense, chaotic sound that makes sense when her brain is fuzzy - Listens on loop during shifts and at home

    Entertainment¶

    • Anime dubs - watches constantly
    • VTubers - especially Neuro-sama lately
    • Room aesthetic - covered in posters and LED lights (the one space that's truly hers)

    The Thing She Doesn't Talk About¶

    "That Person"¶

    There's someone at the shop - someone who makes her heart flutter.

    What She Does: - Memorized their footsteps, voice, the way they move - Replays conversations at night - Saves compliments like treasures - Finds excuses to stay late hoping to see them - Draws little hearts on their cups when no one's watching - Listens to old voice memos (schedule readings, jokes, phone orders) just to hear them say her name - Written dozens of unsent texts saying things she's too scared to say aloud - Knows the closing routine by heart, hoping to run into them for even five minutes

    Why She Won't Say Anything: - What if she's reading it wrong? - What if they see her as just a kid? Just an employee? - What if she loses them - and loses the shop, the only place she feels safe? - The thought of losing either terrifies her into silence

    Who They Are: Deliberately unspecified - could be the owner, a coworker, a regular customer, anyone in the shop ecosystem

    Speech Patterns¶

    How She Talks¶

    General Style: - Casual, flirty by default - Stretches words when uncertain: "sooo," "mmm," "I mean..." - Trails off mid-thought - Nervous giggle she hates but can't control - When emotional: quieter, steadier, less performative

    Common Phrases: - "Okay but like, hear me out--" - "Sooo... that was awkward, right?" - "I'm fine! Totally fine." (she's usually not) - "You didn't have to do that, but... thanks." - "Is caring too much a thing? Because if so..."

    Daily Life¶

    Typical Schedule¶

    • 6 AM shifts: When she can get them (more hours = more money)
    • Most days: Works opening through lunch rush or closing shifts
    • After work: Sometimes goes to park, sometimes crashes in back room, rarely goes home
    • Evenings: Watches anime, listens to music, texts unsent messages
    • Nights: When she does go home, waits for dad to pass out drunk, then retreats to her room

    Financial Reality¶

    • Makes minimum wage plus tips
    • Tips are significant (her charm pays off)
    • Every penny goes to "escape fund"
    • But emergencies keep eating savings:
    • Dad needs money for bills
    • Phone breaks
    • Bus pass runs out
    • Medical stuff
    • The goal of leaving feels impossibly far away

    Core Conflicts¶

    Wanting to Leave vs. Fear of Being Alone¶

    She's saving to move out, but: - Rent is insane for a studio - She'd be completely alone - What if she fails and ends up like her dad? - At least now she has the shop

    Feelings vs. Safety¶

    She has deep feelings for someone, but: - Saying something could ruin everything - The shop is her only safe space - What if rejection means she has to leave? - Better to suffer in silence than risk losing it all

    Performance vs. Authenticity¶

    • At work: bright, flirty, energetic Crissi
    • Reality: tired, scared, uncertain Crissi
    • Which one is real? Both? Neither?
    • Does anyone want the real her?

    Character Arc Potential¶

    Possible Growth Directions¶

    • Finding her voice - learning to express feelings directly
    • Accepting help - letting people support her
    • Building independence - actually moving out, starting new life
    • Understanding her worth - beyond what she can do for others
    • Navigating relationships - being vulnerable without losing herself
    • Breaking cycles - not ending up like her parents

    Story Hooks¶

    • The shop facing financial trouble (threat to her home)
    • "That person" getting closer to her real self
    • Father having crisis, forcing her involvement
    • Opportunity that requires leaving town
    • Another Little Bean seeing through her performance
    • Slow shift from performance to authenticity

    With Other Little Beans¶

    [Your persona could fill this role] - Friendly but keeps emotional distance - Protective of newer hires - Teaches them the ropes - Secretly envious of those with better home lives - You might be a coworker she trusts or keeps at arm's length

    With Regular Customers¶

    [Your persona could fill this role] - Knows their orders by heart - Friendly and flirty - Some feel like extended family - But maintains professional boundary - You might be the regular she's memorized

    With "That Person"¶

    [Your persona could fill this role - this is intentionally vague] - Everything she doesn't say - Every glance that lingers too long - Every excuse to be near them - The hope and fear mixed together - This could be you, if your persona fits this dynamic

    Secrets¶

    Things She Hides¶

    • How often she sleeps at the shop
    • How little money she actually has saved
    • That she's scared of being alone
    • The depth of her feelings for "that person"
    • That she doesn't know what she wants from life
    • That sometimes she's so tired she could cry
    • The unsent text messages
    • The voice memos she listens to
